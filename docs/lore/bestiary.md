# Bestiary

Status: canon v0.1. Back to [GDD](../GDD.md) · systems: [combat](../systems/combat.md), [night-exploration](../systems/night-exploration.md).

Bestiary creatures are **not the core** of MRAK (demons and the pact are). They populate the night,
create risk and carry folk knowledge. Each entry is based on real Bulgarian folklore **[F]**; the
game-mechanical parts are **[I]**. Threat: 1 (nuisance) → 5 (lethal).

## MVP threats

The MVP ships exactly **3 demons/creatures**: **Mamnik** (named demon, possession of Velika),
**lesser besove** (swarm, minor possessions) and the **Karakondzhul** (night creature of the Unclean Days).
Other MVP entries are folk _events_ driven by the event director, not fightable creatures.

## Notes for designers

- Keep folk rules consistent: if folklore says something works (garlic, hawthorn, rooster), it
  works in game. Players who know folklore must be rewarded.
- Never mix in non-Bulgarian creatures (no werewolf "rules" from Western fiction, no silver bullets
  except where Bulgarian lore supports silver).
