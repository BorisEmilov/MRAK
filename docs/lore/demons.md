# Demons

Status: canon v0.1. Back to [GDD](../GDD.md) · systems: [possession](../systems/possession.md), [exorcism](../systems/exorcism.md), [pacts](../systems/pacts.md).

Legend: **[F]** real folklore · **[I]** invented for MRAK.

## Folk basis [F]

- In Bulgarian, a demon is a **bes** (plural **besove**). The devil is rarely named aloud; folk
  speech uses euphemisms: _nechistiya_ ("the unclean one"), _rogatiya_ ("the horned one"),
  _toy_ ("him"), _dyavolat_.
- Unclean forces are strongest at night, at crossroads, mills, abandoned wells and ruins, and
  between Christmas and Theophany (the Unclean Days).
- Protection: the sign of the cross, incense, holy water from Theophany, garlic, iron, red thread,
  tar crosses on doors, bread and salt, the church bell, the first cock's crow.

## The hierarchy [I]

```
                The Horned One (Rogatiya) — signs pacts, never possesses directly
                           │
        Named demons (9) — possess hosts, have true names and seals
                           │
     Lesser besove — nameless, swarm; cause noise, bad luck, small symptoms
```

- **True name**: required for the Bind phase of an [exorcism](../systems/exorcism.md). Learned from
  grimoire pages, the possessed's raving, the forest spirit (at a price) or folk tales.
- **Seal**: a sigil engraved on gear or drawn in chalk/ash; required to bind. Each seal has a
  _form_ (shape) and a _medium_ (what it must be drawn with).
- **Rank**: Lesser (1), Named (2–3), Prince (4). Rank adds Shadow dice to exorcism checks.
- The forces hate each other: named demons covet the Horned One's pacts; the forest spirit
  devours demons that enter her forest; the Thracian Horseman ignores all of them — and they fear him.

## The Horned One — _Rogatiya_ [I, from folk epithet]

- **True name:** never revealed in the main story; the pure route reveals that he has none —
  "a name is a contract, and he signs only others'."
- **Form:** a well-dressed traveller at the crossroads whose shadow has horns. Never enters a church
  or a house uninvited.
- **Wants:** contracts. Souls are a currency, not a goal.
- **Weakness:** the exact wording of his own clauses; a pact read aloud in full in a church loses
  one hidden clause.
- **Rule:** never possesses; acts through [pacts](../systems/pacts.md) and debt collectors.

## Named demons [I]

All nine are invented, built from folk motifs (the luring water spirit, the hunger in the granary,
the evil eye, nightmare-riding). Symptoms progress through stages 1–5 (see
[possession](../systems/possession.md)).

## Lesser besove [I]

Nameless swarm (rank 1). They cause [event director](../systems/event-director.md) noise: knocks,
spilled milk, lost tools, lamp flicker. A lesser bes can still possess a sick or terrified
villager (Fear ≥ 80), with symptoms up to stage 3. Its "true name" is generic: _bes_. Banished with
a stage-1 exorcism (no seal required).
