# The Zlatev bloodline of Zlatno

Status: canon v0.1 · **Entirely invented [I]**. Back to [GDD](../GDD.md) · [timeline](timeline.md).

Zlatno ("golden") is a village one valley east of Mrak. It has stone houses, a church with a
silver-covered iconostasis, fat flocks and a fountain that never freezes. Its chorbadzhii (notables)
lend money to half the district. Travellers call it blessed. Mrak calls it lucky and hates it for it.

## The secret

The founder, **Dragan Zlatev**, signed the Pact with the Horned One at the crossroads in 1779.

- **Terms (known to the head of the house):** Zlatno prospers while the Pact is renewed.
- **Price (hidden from most of the family):** Mrak pays — failed harvests, fevers, possessions,
  and one life given each generation ("the tithe").
- **Renewal:** the eldest heir renews the Pact on their first **Enyovden** as head of house, with
  blood and an object taken from Mrak (bread, hair, a grave's soil).
- **Hidden clause (only the Horned One knows):** if the Pact is ever renewed by an heir who does
  not believe in it, the debt transfers to Zlatno. This is what the "steal the pact" route exploits.

## Rivalry with the Svetlevi

Each generation, the active [guardian](guardian-bloodline.md) has a Zlatev counterpart (the
**Rival**). The Rival's attitude (hostile, wary, secretly allied) is a WorldState value carried
across generations: insults, debts and favours are remembered by both houses.

## Historical framing

The Zlatevi are chorbadzhii: Christian notables who mediated between villages and Ottoman
authorities, collected taxes and lent money. Historically the chorbadzhii were neither uniformly
collaborators nor heroes; many funded schools and churches. MRAK keeps that ambiguity: Zlatno's
wealth also builds a school, and many of its people are kind and innocent of the Pact.
