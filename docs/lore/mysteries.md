# Mysteries

Status: canon v0.1 · **all invented [I]** on folk motifs. Back to [GDD](../GDD.md) · system: [mysteries](../systems/mysteries.md).

Every mystery is solved on the **deduction board**: the player links clues to propositions and
commits a **conclusion** = _culprit_ + _cause_ + _remedy_. A wrong culprit **condemns an innocent**
(the village acts on the guardian's word). Clue IDs are stable content keys.

---

## M1 — The Wasting Bride (MVP · The Unclean Days, nights 2–12)

**Hook.** Night 2: the church bell rings at **3:33** though the tower is locked. At dawn Velika the
weaver will not wake; then she sleepwalks toward the samodivi well, soaked, with river weeds in her
hair, three days before her wedding.

**Question.** _Who brought the evil on Velika, and how is it cast out?_

**Truth.** On Christmas Eve, Chorbadzhi Dimo buried a **magiya** (a sorcery bundle: Velika's hair,
grave soil, a rusty nail and a Zlatno gold coin) under her family's threshold, on Kostadin Zlatev's
order — Mrak's "bride" for this generation's tithe. The bundle opened a door for **Mamnik, the
Lurer**. Dimo did it to cancel his debt to Zlatno.

**Suspects.**

| Suspect    | Why they look guilty                                                               | Why they are not                                                                       |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Baba Neda  | Her charm is found sewn in Velika's sleeve; "the old witch".                       | The charm is protective — it is what has kept Velika alive. Accusing her repeats 1779. |
| Krastyo    | Seen near Velika's house at night; flour on her windowsill; the mill is "unclean". | He was teaching her to read the carvings at the mill.                                  |
| Radka      | Quarrelled with Velika's mother; her rope is found at the well.                    | The rope was stolen from the tavern by a sleepwalking Velika.                          |
| **Dimo** ✔ | Hides it well: respected, pious, helpful in the search.                            | —                                                                                      |

**Clues.**

| ID            | Clue                                                                   | Found by                              | Points to                           |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| m1.bell       | Bell rope wet and smelling of river water                              | Night 2, bell tower (Investigation)   | demon of water                      |
| m1.prints     | Wet footprints leading from the threshold, not to it                   | Velika's house (Investigation)        | something came _from_ the threshold |

| m1.rope       | Tavern rope at the well, knot tied left-handed (Velika is left-handed) | Forest well (Investigation)           | clears Radka                        |

**Solution.** Culprit **Dimo** · cause **magiya + Mamnik** · remedy **burn the magiya, exorcise with
the true name and seal before Theophany dawn**.

**Outcomes.**

- **Correct + exorcism succeeds:** Velika lives; wedding at Theophany (+Village morale, +Revelation 30).
  Dimo confesses and flees to Zlatno → the aga appoints a new tax collector (event chain); first hard
  evidence of the Pact (the Zlatno coin) enters the journal.
- **Correct + exorcism fails:** Mamnik jumps (to the most frightened villager, or the guardian on a bad
  omen). Velika lives but is "marked" (sickness).
- **Wrong: Baba Neda** → the mob takes her to the forest edge. Unless the guardian stops it
  (Leadership check at Hysteria penalty) she is **burned** like Stana: herbalist's hut lost,
  forest spirit becomes **hostile** for the generation, village Corruption +20, Lazhets gains a host.
  Velika dies at Theophany.
- **Wrong: Krastyo** → handed to the aga's men "for sorcery and debts"; the mill stops (food −30%
  for the rest of the chapter). Velika dies unless exorcised anyway (remedy without culprit: −2 Fate dice).
- **Wrong: Radka** → the tavern closes; haiduti contacts lost (blocks a full-game route branch).
- **Unsolved by night 12:** Velika walks into the water at dawn of Theophany.

Hanging or burning anyone is a crime under Ottoman law: any lynching triggers the aga's inquiry
(fines, arrests, reputation loss with the aga) — the world reacts, not only the village.

## M1b — The Girl Without a Shadow (MVP · The Unclean Days, from night 3)

Mara, Radka's daughter, casts no shadow by the lamp and fades. **Truth:** Krastyo the miller,
in debt to Dimo for the new weir, measured her shadow with a red thread at dusk and built it
into the weir (_vgradena senka_ [F]) so the wall would stand. **Remedy:** break open the weir and
burn the thread. **Red herrings:** Baba Neda's red threads (refuted by Velika's alibi); the
samodivi's dance ring (doubtful); Pop Stoyan's testimony against the stranger Todor — a lie to
keep Todor away from the register that hides Zlatno's silver (retracted when his secret comes
out). Wrong answers cost Mara's life and brand an innocent; half right (the miller confronted,
the thread left in the wall) still loses her.

## M2 — The Empty Graves (full game · Zadushnitsa)

**Hook.** Families bringing kolivo to their dead find three graves sunken and empty.

**Question.** _Who is taking Mrak's dead, and why?_

**Truth.** Every generation the Pact requires "the bones of Mrak under Zlatno's gold": Zlatno's men
carry them to the mine and lay them where **Kopach** digs toward the Horseman. Petko unknowingly
helped in 1829, believing he reburied plague dead.

**Suspects.** a _vampir_ (folk explanation — a recent improper burial), **Petko** the gravedigger,
the **haiduti** (who do use one crypt as a cache), the **monastery's last monk** (alive, hiding in the ruins).

**Key clues.** sunken graves facing east · cart tracks with Zlatno's iron-rimmed wheels · haiduti
cache with arms but no bones · Petko's silver coins minted 1829 · the monk's register of 1779 burials ·
bones arranged in Thracian patterns in the mine · daguerreotype of the cemetery showing figures with lanterns (only if the chapter falls in 1858 or later).

**Solution.** Culprit **the Zlatno grave-robbers led by the Rival's steward** · cause **the Pact's
tithe of the dead** · remedy **reconsecrate the graves and seal the mine passage**.

**Wrong accusations.**

- **"Vampire":** the village stakes and burns the body of a recently dead villager — desecration,
  Corruption +15, the dead man's family turns against the guardian; the theft continues.
- **Petko:** he is beaten and exiled; burials stop being done properly → a real vampire rises next chapter.
- **Haiduti:** the aga is informed, soldiers come; the haiduti retaliate; route "ally with the
  mountains" closes.
- **The monk:** he dies in custody; his register (key to M3) is lost.

## M3 — The Golden Harvest (full game · Enyovden, spans two generations)

**Hook.** Zlatno's barley is gold while Mrak's rots in the same rain. A Zlatno child, lost in the
forest, says "grandfather gave the man at the crossroads our bad luck".

**Question.** _Why does Zlatno prosper?_

**Truth.** The 1779 Pact (see [zlatno-bloodline](zlatno-bloodline.md)), renewed every generation
at Enyovden.

**Competing explanations (suspect propositions).** a miraculous icon in Zlatno's church · a hidden
silver vein · trade privileges bought from the aga · **the Pact** ✔.

**Key clues.** Dragan's cipher ledger in the altar table · Stana's last words (prologue) ·
the forest spirit's memory (costs a memory of the guardian) · the Rival's private confession
(affinity ≥ 50) · the crossroads ashes after Enyovden · the hidden clause wording (only via the
Horned One or the pure route).

**Solution.** The Pact exists and its renewal is due this Enyovden → opens the **route choice**
(expose / break / steal / ally with the forest / serve the Horned One / pure).

**Wrong conclusions.**

- **Icon:** the guardian steals or damages the icon — sacrilege, Faith −30, Zlatno's innocent
  villagers suffer, nothing changes.
- **Silver vein:** Mrak digs into the mine → wakes Kopach early (prince demon released).
- **Aga's privileges:** a petition accusing the aga reaches Plovdiv; the aga's response falls on
  Mrak (taxes +50% for a year). Historically plausible, and devastating.

## Writing rules for mysteries

1. Every suspect must have a **true innocent explanation** that a careful player can find.
2. The real culprit must be **provable from clues alone**, without supernatural certainty.
3. Clues may be noise or hallucinations (pillar 4) — but the solution chain never depends on a hallucination.
4. A wrong conclusion is a story, not a game over.
