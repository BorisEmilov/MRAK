# Glossary

Status: v0.1. Back to [GDD](../GDD.md) · style: [writing-style](../writing-style.md).

Transliteration follows the Bulgarian official system (2009), simplified: **zh** = ж, **ts** = ц,
**ch** = ч, **sh** = ш, **sht** = щ, **a** for ъ, **y** for й, **yu** = ю, **ya** = я.
**[F]** folklore · **[H]** history · **[I]** invented for MRAK.

## History and society

| Term                   | Meaning                                                                                        | Tag |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| **aga**                | Ottoman local notable/landholder with administrative power; here the district's tax authority. | H   |
| **bedel-i askeri**     | Military-exemption tax paid by non-Muslims after 1856.                                         | H   |
| **chitalishte**        | Community reading room (from 1856).                                                            | H   |
| **chorbadzhiya**       | Christian village notable, often tax intermediary and lender.                                  | H   |
| **cizye** (jizya)      | Poll tax on non-Muslim subjects, until 1856.                                                   | H   |
| **daskal**             | Teacher.                                                                                       | H   |
| **grosh** (kuruş)      | Silver piastre, the main coin; 40 para = 1 grosh.                                              | H   |
| **haidut, haiduti**    | Armed outlaws/rebels of the mountains; folk heroes and feared.                                 | H   |
| **kadı**               | Ottoman judge; the only lawful authority for capital cases.                                    | H   |
| **kiliyno uchilishte** | Cell school in a church or monastery annex.                                                    | H   |
| **klisar**             | Church sexton.                                                                                 | H   |
| **kmet**               | Village headman.                                                                               | H   |
| **konak**              | Mansion/administrative residence.                                                              | H   |
| **mehana**             | Tavern.                                                                                        | H   |
| **Pomaks**             | Bulgarian-speaking Muslims, many living in the Rhodopes.                                       | H   |
| **sancak**             | Ottoman province subdivision (Mrak lies in the sancak of Filibe/Plovdiv).                      | H   |
| **Tanzimat**           | Ottoman reform era, 1839–1876.                                                                 | H   |
| **voivoda**            | Leader of a haidut band.                                                                       | H   |
| **yatak**              | A haidut's hidden supporter who gives shelter and food.                                        | H   |
| **zaptiye**            | Ottoman gendarmerie (from the 1840s).                                                          | H   |
