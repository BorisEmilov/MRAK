# Timeline

Status: canon v0.1. Back to [GDD](../GDD.md) · see also [glossary](glossary.md).

Legend: **[H]** historical fact · **[F]** real folklore / folk practice · **[I]** invented for MRAK.

> Rule: historical entries are presented as context, never as caricature. Ottoman officials,
> Muslim neighbours (including the Bulgarian-speaking Muslim Pomaks of the Rhodopes) and the
> clergy are people with interests, fears and faith of their own — not villains by default.

## Deep past

| Date             | Event                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Tag |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| c. 1st mill. BC  | Thracian tribes inhabit the Rhodopes. Rock sanctuaries and fortresses are carved into the mountain (cf. Perperikon, Tatul).                                                                                   | [H] |
| c. 2nd–4th c. AD | The cult of the **Thracian Horseman** (Heros) flourishes: hundreds of votive reliefs show a rider with a spear or a hunt.                                                                                     | [H] |
| Unknown          | Beneath the hill that will become Mrak's mine, a Thracian sanctuary seals away "the Rider who does not ride out". The Horseman of MRAK is neither god nor demon: something older that the later powers avoid. | [I] |
| 1371–1396        | Ottoman conquest of the Bulgarian lands.                                                                                                                                                                      | [H] |
| 16th–17th c.     | The Rhodopes become a mosaic of Christian Bulgarian and Pomak (Muslim Bulgarian-speaking) villages, sharing language, songs, trades and many folk beliefs.                                                    | [H] |
| 1640s            | The mine above Mrak is worked for silver and lead under Ottoman concession; it is abandoned after a collapse that kills thirty miners.                                                                        | [I] |

## The founding wrong (prologue era)

| Date          | Event                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Tag |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| 1768–1774     | Russo-Ottoman War; hardship and requisitions across the Balkans.                                                                                                                                                                        | [H] |
| Spring 1779   | Famine in Mrak and Zlatno. Dragan Zlatev of Zlatno goes to the crossroads at midnight and signs the **Pact** with the Horned One: prosperity for Zlatno, paid by Mrak.                                                                  | [I] |
| Summer 1779   | **Stana**, a widowed herb-woman of Mrak, sees Dragan return from the crossroads with blood on his sleeve. Mrak's cattle sicken; Dragan names Stana as the witch.                                                                        | [I] |
| Enyovden 1779 | A mob burns Stana at the forest edge. Not a trial: witch-burning was never an Ottoman or Orthodox legal institution. It is an extra-legal killing that both villages bury in silence. **Prologue: the player plays Stana's last days.** | [I] |
| Same night    | Ivan Svetlev, who carried the torch, takes in Stana's infant daughter Kalina "out of Christian duty". The Svetlev bloodline begins carrying both the guilt and the blood.                                                               | [I] |
| 1780s         | Zlatno's fields yield double. Mrak's wells sour; its children die of fevers. Stana's spirit is first reported in the Samodivi Forest.                                                                                                   | [I] |
| 1790s–1810s   | **Kardzhalii** period: armed bands ravage the countryside; central authority weakens.                                                                                                                                                   | [H] |
| 1811          | Dragan Zlatev dies; his son Hristo renews the Pact on his first Enyovden as head of the house.                                                                                                                                          | [I] |
| 1826          | The Janissary corps is abolished in Istanbul.                                                                                                                                                                                           | [H] |
| 1831–1834     | The Ottoman timar (fief) system is dismantled; tax collection passes through tax farmers and local notables.                                                                                                                            | [H] |

## The guardian era (playable, 1834 → 1878)

| Date           | Event                                                                                                                                                                   | Tag |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| Christmas 1834 | **Game start.** The first Svetlev guardian is chosen by the village after the old guardian's death. Chapter 1: _The Unclean Days_ (12 nights, MVP).                     | [I] |
| 1835           | Kostadin Zlatev renews the Pact. The Monastery of St. Elijah above Mrak loses its last monk.                                                                            | [I] |
| 1835           | The first Bulgarian secular school opens in Gabrovo (1835) — the schooling movement spreads to villages through the decades.                                            | [H] |
| 1839           | **Edict of Gülhane** opens the Tanzimat reforms (equality of subjects before the law promised).                                                                         | [H] |
| 1839           | The daguerreotype is announced in Paris. In the Bulgarian lands photography arrives only in the 1850s–60s, through travelling photographers.                            | [H] |
| 1840s          | Tax farming and local abuses persist despite reform; haiduti remain active in the Rhodopes.                                                                             | [H] |
| 1856           | **Reform Edict (Islahat)**; the poll tax on non-Muslims (cizye) is replaced by the military-exemption tax (bedel-i askeri).                                             | [H] |
| 1856 onwards   | Reading rooms (chitalishta) spread across Bulgarian towns.                                                                                                              | [H] |
| 1858 (in game) | First daguerreotypes in Mrak: a travelling photographer from Plovdiv. Some plates show figures nobody saw.                                                              | [I] |
| 1860           | The Bulgarian Church question: Plovdiv and other towns stop commemorating the Greek Patriarch.                                                                          | [H] |
| 1870           | **Firman establishing the Bulgarian Exarchate.**                                                                                                                        | [H] |
| 1876           | **April Uprising**, suppressed with mass violence (incl. Batak, western Rhodopes). Handled in game with gravity, from the villagers' point of view, never as spectacle. | [H] |
| 1877–1878      | Russo-Ottoman War; Treaty of San Stefano and Congress of Berlin. The Rhodopes are divided. **End of the main chronicle's historical frame.**                            | [H] |
| After 1878     | **Endless Chronicle** continues with procedural years; history is referenced, never re-enacted in detail.                                                               | [I] |

## Calendar note

In-world dates use the **Julian (Old Style)** calendar, as the Bulgarian Church did in the 19th
century (12 days behind the Gregorian calendar). Real-world seasonal events
([seasonal-events](../systems/seasonal-events.md)) use modern Gregorian dates.
