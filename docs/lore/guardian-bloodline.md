# The Svetlev bloodline (the guardians)

Status: canon v0.1 · **Invented [I]**. Back to [GDD](../GDD.md) · systems: [lineage](../systems/lineage.md), [bloodline-tree](../systems/bloodline-tree.md).

"Svetlev" comes from _svetlo_, light. In Mrak the Svetlevi have kept the watch at night for as long
as anyone remembers: they ring the bell, keep the lamp at the chapel, walk the fences during the
Unclean Days. The village calls the current one simply **the Guardian** (_Pazitelya_).

## Origin

- **Ivan Svetlev** (1750–1806) carried the torch at Stana's burning in 1779. That same night he and
  his wife **Mara** took in Stana's infant daughter, **Kalina**, and raised her as their own.
- Kalina married Ivan's son. **Every Svetlev since then carries Stana's blood** — and the guilt
  of her murderer. Nobody alive knows this at game start; it is the core revelation of the pure route.
- The watch began as Ivan's penance. It became the family's trade.

## What the bloodline inherits

| Inherited                                            | Not inherited                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Pacts signed, **Debt**, curses                       | Talents (knowledge is not blood)                             |
| Heirlooms (lantern, icon, knife, journal)            | Skill ranks (an heir starts with their calling's)            |
| The **Bloodline Tree** (account-level)               | Personal Sanity, Faith, Corruption (reset ±)                 |
| The **Journal** — the only way knowledge passes down | Relationships (villagers remember the house, not the person) |
| Village reputation of the house                      |                                                              |

## Family traits (examples, via the Bloodline Tree)

- **Stana's Eyes** (unlocked only after learning the truth): see the forest spirit's marks.
- **Light-Keepers**: lantern burns 10% longer.
- **The Torch's Shame**: +Resolve against the forest spirit's anger, −Presence with her.
