# Callings

Status: canon v0.1 · mechanics **[I]**, inspired by career systems in tabletop RPGs (adapted, not
copied). Back to [GDD](../GDD.md) · systems: [callings](../systems/callings.md), [skills](../systems/skills.md), [talents](../systems/talents.md) · trees: [talent-trees](talent-trees.md).

A **calling** is what a guardian _is_ before they take the watch. It sets:

- **+1 to two attributes** (on top of the base 2 in each; the player adds +1 anywhere).
- **4 calling skills** (v1 has 10 skills, see [skills](../systems/skills.md)): start with rank cap 2 (others cap 1) and cost less to raise
  ([skills](../systems/skills.md)).
- **3 starting skill ranks** (rank 1 in three calling skills).
- **2 specialization trees** (one chosen free at start; the other bought later).
- **Starting gear** and a **calling ability**.
- A **default virtue and sin** suggestion ([virtue-sin](../systems/virtue-sin.md)); the player may change them.

## Priest's Acolyte

**Fantasy.** You served at the altar before you could read; you know the liturgy by heart and
believe — mostly. The village expects you to stand between them and the dark with a censer and a cross.

- **Starting ranks:** Faith 1, Exorcism 1, Lore 1.
- **Calling ability — _Kyrie_:** once per night, a prayer restores 15 Sanity and cancels one bad omen.
- **Gear:** censer and incense (3), wooden cross (amulet), small vial of holy water (2), lantern.
- **Virtue / sin (default):** Mercy / Pride.
- **Plays like:** exorcisms, calming hysteria, strong vs demons, weak in fights.

## Znaharka

**Fantasy.** The village calls for you when a child won't stop crying, when a cow's milk turns to
blood, when the priest has given up. You know which herb is picked at dawn and which word is
whispered three times. Some call you a witch behind your back. (The calling name is the
traditional feminine word; any guardian can follow it.)

- **Starting ranks:** Herbalism 1, Survival 1, Occultism 1.
- **Calling ability — _Leene na kurshum_ (lead pouring):** diagnose fright in a villager — reveals
  their Fear source and whether something unnatural is involved (not which).
- **Gear:** herb satchel (5 slots), lead and ladle, red thread (3), knife, lantern.
- **Virtue / sin (default):** Compassion / Wrath.
- **Plays like:** healing, alchemy, charms, best village support; risks being accused of witchcraft
  (+suspicion in hysteria).

## Hunter

**Fantasy.** You know the forest paths and the sound a wolf makes before it attacks. You have seen
tracks no animal leaves.

- **Starting ranks:** Combat 1, Survival 1, Stealth 1.
- **Calling ability — _Read the Trail_:** once per night, reveal the nearest creature and its type.
- **Gear:** flintlock rifle (old _shishane_), powder (10), hunting knife, snare (2), lantern.
- **Virtue / sin:** Courage / Cruelty.

## Scholar

**Fantasy.** You studied at a monastery school or in Plovdiv; you read Greek and Church Slavonic
and you have opened books that should have been burned.

- **Starting ranks:** Lore 1, Investigation 1, Occultism 1.
- **Calling ability — _Marginalia_:** journal capacity +4 pages; one free grimoire page translation per chapter.
- **Gear:** books (2), ink and quill, magnifying glass, candles (4), lantern.
- **Virtue / sin:** Truthfulness / Curiosity (the sin of knowing too much — raises Notice).

## Haidut

**Fantasy.** You came down from the mountains with a price on your head. You fight dirty, move
silently, and you have friends in every tavern loft.

- **Starting ranks:** Combat 1, Stealth 1, Persuasion 1.
- **Calling ability — _Yatak_:** safe houses: once per night, hide in a village house to skip
  time to dawn without the dawn penalty.
- **Gear:** yataghan (blade), pistol (_kubura_), powder (6), cloak, lantern.
- **Virtue / sin:** Loyalty / Vengefulness.
- **Note:** a known haidut guardian raises the aga's attention (tax and inquiry events).

## Gravedigger

**Fantasy.** You have buried half the village and you know how the dead lie. You are not afraid of
the cemetery — you are afraid of what is _not_ in it.

- **Starting ranks:** Survival 1, Faith 1, Lore 1.
- **Calling ability — _Proper Rites_:** burials you perform never produce vampires; the dead buried
  by you may answer one question on Zadushnitsa.
- **Gear:** spade (weapon), hawthorn stakes (2), salt (3), bell, lantern.
- **Virtue / sin:** Patience / Greed.
