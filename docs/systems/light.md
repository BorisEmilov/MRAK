# Light

Status: v1 · MVP: ✅ · Package: `packages/core/src/world/light.ts` + Phaser lighting (`apps/game/src/game/world/`) · Back to [GDD](../GDD.md) · [index](README.md)

## Purpose

**Light is life and ammunition.** It protects sanity, reveals the path, repels creatures — and
every burst spent is light you won't have at 4 a.m.

## Data

- Sources: lantern (oil), candles, torches, censer, hearths, fixed lantern posts, moonlight.
- `light: { oil, radius, sources[], moon: full|waxing|new|pulled-down, darknessLevel }`.

## Rules

1. Lantern consumes oil continuously (1 unit / 15 in-world minutes). Base radius 4 tiles.
2. **Light burst**: 1 oil → radius ×2 flash: stuns creatures, reveals hidden marks for 3 s.
3. **Darkness** (outside any light): Sanity drain ([sanity](sanity.md)), +1 Shadow die, creature
   aggression +.
4. **Moon phase** adds ambient light outdoors (full: +2 radius, new: 0).
5. **"Pulling down the moon"** — Rhodope folk belief that witches can bring the moon down from the
   sky [F]. In game the forest spirit can cause a **pitch-black night**: ambient 0, lantern radius −50%.
6. Fixed lights in the village (chapel lamp, watchtower fire) form **safe zones** — no creature
   spawns inside; villagers' fear gain −50%.
7. Oil is a village resource: buying, rendering tallow, the chapel's allocation.

## Initial formulas

- Radius = base 4 + blessings + moon bonus; multiplied by 0.5 under a pulled-down moon.
- A night in winter (≈ 630 min) needs ~42 oil units for continuous light: the guardian **cannot**
  keep the lantern lit all night with a standard 20-unit allocation — choices required.

## Events

- **Emits:** `light/oilChanged`, `light/burstUsed`, `light/darknessTick`, `light/moonPulledDown`.
- **Consumes:** `time/*`, `gear/*`, `pact/*`, `forest/*`.

## Edge cases

- Oil runs out mid-burst → burst does not fire (no partial).
- Lantern dropped (karakondzhul) → stays lit on the ground for its remaining fuel; can be retaken.

## Implementation (v1)

### State (WorldState `light`)

`{ lantern: gearId | null, lit: boolean, fuel: 0..1, inDarkness: boolean, darkMinutes: number }`.
`fuel` is what is left of the oil unit poured into the lantern; reserves are `lamp-oil` items in
the inventory (the HUD "light" counter).

### Rules

1. **Equip:** `equipLantern { gear }` takes a gear with slot `lantern` (or `null` to stow it;
   stowing puts it out). A new chronicle equips the start's lantern (`data/starts`).
2. **Light / put out:** `setLanternLit { lit }`. Lighting needs a lantern and either `fuel > 0` or
   one `lamp-oil` (poured in: `fuel = 1`). Rejected otherwise ("no oil").
3. **Burn:** while lit, every in-world minute burns `rate / 15` fuel (`rate` = effective
   `oil-consumption-rate`). When `fuel` reaches 0 the next unit is poured automatically; with no
   unit left the lantern **goes out** (`light/lanternOut`). 60 minutes = 4 units at rate 1.
4. **Radius (tiles)** = `lightRadius` of the gear + `round(2 × moon illumination)` at dusk/night
   outdoors; × 0.5 under a pulled-down moon (flag `moon-pulled-down`), after all bonuses.
5. **Ambient light** (0–1, presentation and rules): day 1; night `0.08 + 0.10 × illumination`;
   dawn/dusk blend smoothly (cosine) over their 60 minutes; a pulled-down moon makes night and
   dusk **0** (only lights remain). Tint: warm by day, amber at dusk, blue-grey at night.
6. **Darkness:** the presentation reports `setInDarkness { dark }` when the guardian stands
   outside every light (lantern, hearths, windows…) while ambient < 0.35. In darkness:
   −1 Sanity every 10 in-world minutes (`light/darknessTick`, [sanity](sanity.md)).
7. `isLit(point, lights)` and `lanternRadius`/`ambientLight` are pure functions shared by core
   tests and the Phaser layer, so the rules and the picture agree.

### Rendering (Phaser 4)

- `scene.lights` with the ambient colour = tint × ambient level; `maxLights` = 32 per camera.
- **Lantern:** a cone light (`Light.setCone`, 4.2+) that turns smoothly towards the facing
  direction (8 directions; aimed at the pointer when the mouse is the active device), plus a
  small spill light around the guardian; flickers; radius from rule 4.
- **Fixed lights** from the map's `light` objects: hearths/bonfires (flicker), candles, windows
  and lamp posts (night only), spirit lights (cold, pulsing).
- **Normal maps** on every tile layer and on the atlases (guardian, props), generated by the
  asset pipeline from the art; all world objects use `setLighting(true)`.
- **Filters** on the camera (respecting [accessibility](../ui/accessibility.md)): sanity →
  desaturation + vignette; low sanity → hallucination distortion (displacement; off with
  reduce motion); night fog (drifting, lit layer); seasonal colour grading; pulled-down moon →
  ambient 0 and a colder, darker grade.

## Test ideas

- Oil consumption over 60 in-world minutes = 4 units.
- Pulled-down moon halves radius after all bonuses.
- Safe zones prevent spawns in seeded runs.
- Burst with 0 oil rejected.
- Lighting with no fuel and no oil is rejected; with 1 unit the lantern burns exactly 15 minutes.
- Ambient: noon = 1, midnight new moon = 0.08, pulled-down = 0; monotonic through dusk.
- Darkness: 60 minutes in darkness = −6 Sanity; in light = 0.
