## Description

_In the Rhodope village of Mrak, every misfortune has a price — and someone else is collecting._

By day you hold a cursed, poor village together; by night you walk the same land transformed,
carrying a lantern that is both your life and your only weapon. Mrak suffers while neighbouring
Zlatno inexplicably prospers. Finding out why — and what you are willing to do about it — takes
generations.

## Features

- Dread, not jump scares: a director paces fear and keeps quiet after every peak.
- Every decision leaves a permanent scar: deaths are final, the village remembers.
- A bloodline that grows across generations of guardians.
- Real, mystery or hallucination — you never know.
- Folklore treated seriously: karakondzhuli, samodivi, kukeri and the rites of the year.
