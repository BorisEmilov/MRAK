## Within 14 days

If you bought the full game and are not happy with it, ask for a refund **within 14 days of
purchase** and we will refund you in full.

## Your legal rights

In the EU and the UK you have a 14-day right of withdrawal for digital content. Because the full
game is delivered immediately, the checkout asks you to agree that delivery starts at once, which
ends the statutory withdrawal right; the voluntary policy above still applies. Your other legal
rights (for example, if the game does not work as described) are not affected.

## How

Write to [support@mrak.game](mailto:support@mrak.game) with your order number, or use the refund
link in the receipt from our payment provider. Refunds go back to the original payment method.
After a refund the full-game content is removed from your account; your demo progress stays.
