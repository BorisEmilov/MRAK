We use **no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies**, so there is no cookie banner.

| Name               | Set by        | Purpose                                                                                                            | Lifetime                                     |
| ------------------ | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| `__Secure-mrak_rt` | api.mrak.game | Keeps you signed in. HttpOnly (scripts cannot read it), Secure, SameSite=Strict, sent only to the sign-in service. | Up to 30 days of inactivity, 90 days at most |

The game also stores your saves and settings **in your browser** (IndexedDB and local storage).
That data stays on your device; clearing your browser data deletes it (sign in to keep a copy in
your account).

Cloudflare Turnstile, which protects sign-up and password recovery from bots, may set its own
strictly necessary technical data while the check runs.
