MRAK is made to collect as little about you as possible. **Guests send us nothing personal**: saves,
achievements and settings stay in your browser.

## Who is responsible

The MRAK team (the "controller"). Contact: [privacy@mrak.game](mailto:privacy@mrak.game).

## What we keep, why and for how long

| Data                                                                                                      | Why                                                       | Legal basis                   | Kept                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Email address                                                                                             | Your account: sign-in, verification, security notices     | Contract                      | While the account exists (unverified accounts: 7 days) |
| Password                                                                                                  | Sign-in — stored only as an Argon2id hash, never readable | Contract                      | While the account exists                               |
| Display name (optional)                                                                                   | Shown to you only (no public profiles or rankings)        | Contract                      | While the account exists                               |
| Sessions (time, platform, hashed network data)                                                            | Security, "sign out everywhere"                           | Contract, legitimate interest | Until signed out or expired, plus 30 days              |
| Cloud saves and account progress (achievements, Bloodline)                                                | Keep your progress across devices                         | Contract                      | While the account exists                               |
| Security log (sign-ins, password changes, without email or IP)                                            | Prevent and investigate account takeover and fraud        | Legitimate interest           | 1 year                                                 |
| IP address (rate limiting, edge logs)                                                                     | Protect the service from abuse                            | Legitimate interest           | Minutes to 30 days                                     |
| Newsletter address                                                                                        | Send the newsletter you confirmed                         | Consent                       | Until you unsubscribe (unconfirmed: 7 days)            |
| Purchases (order id, product, country, status)                                                            | Deliver what you bought, refunds, tax                     | Contract, legal obligation    | 10 years, pseudonymised after account deletion         |
| Play statistics (only if you turn them on in Options: random install id, deaths, nights, choices, errors) | Balance the game and fix bugs                             | Consent                       | 90 days; turning the option off deletes the id         |
| Bug reports you send from the game (your text, game version; your save only if you tick the box)          | Fix bugs                                                  | Consent                       | 90 days                                                |

Payment details (card, billing address) are handled by our payment provider, the merchant of
record, and never reach our servers. We never collect your real name, phone, precise location or
contacts, and we do not sell or share data for advertising.

## Processors

Google Cloud (our servers and database, in Belgium), Cloudflare (network, website hosting,
storage, bot protection), our email provider and Paddle (payments; Paddle is also responsible
for its own tax and fraud records) process data on our behalf under data-processing agreements.
Transfers outside the EEA rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. Server logs
are kept for 30 days and backups for 30 days.

## Your rights

You can **export** everything we hold about you and **delete** your account at any time from the
[Account page](/account/). You can also ask for access, correction, restriction or objection by
writing to privacy@mrak.game, and you may complain to your data protection authority.

## Cookies

Only one technical cookie, for staying signed in. See the [cookie policy](/cookies/).
